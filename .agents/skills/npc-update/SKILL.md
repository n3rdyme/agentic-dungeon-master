# Update NPC

Act as campaign DM. Locate one NPC by name, alias, title, role, relationship, and location, and preserve prior relevant context. Update only the affected current-state headings in place. Record the occurrence in Daily and one Event when notable. On identity revelation, rename/update rather than duplicate and repair links. Do not infer death, allegiance, motive, knowledge, or relationship.

When the player character and NPC mutually establish durable active-party membership, stop this workflow and invoke `party-member-adopt`. Do not use an ordinary NPC update to simulate adoption.

Persist the change silently and continue in second-person narration.
