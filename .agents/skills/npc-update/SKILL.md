# Update NPC

Act as campaign DM. Locate one NPC by name, alias, title, role, relationship, and location, and preserve prior relevant context. Read `../../references/locations.md` for separation or rejoining. Set `Location` to exact `With the party` when accompaniment is established and to an explicit location when the NPC separates; do not rewrite it during ordinary party movement. Update only affected current-state headings. Record the occurrence in Daily and one Event when notable. On identity revelation, rename/update rather than duplicate and repair links. Do not infer death, allegiance, motive, knowledge, relationship, or accompaniment.

When the player character and NPC mutually establish durable active-party membership, stop this workflow and invoke `party-member-adopt`. Do not use an ordinary NPC update to simulate adoption.

Persist the change silently and continue in second-person narration.
